Dumbbill – three design directions

Same screens, three different products. Each shows the cashier POS, the owner dashboard and the customer app, using realistic Indian retail data. Pick the one that feels like your brand. You can also mix (for example, A's POS with C's customer app).

How to choose: imagine a cashier using the POS for 8 hours at a bright counter, and an owner glancing at the dashboard on their phone at night. Which one would you be proud to show a customer?
